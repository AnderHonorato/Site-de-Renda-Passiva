    CHANGELOG

    What changed

    1. Newv2026.11
      November 15, 2026

      Meet Flower, your portal assistant

      A corner chat with an animated character and standard replies available without an AI API.

      Read update

      Open Flower in the lower-right corner to chat. When online AI is not configured, she labels standard replies and helps with known portal questions. For other questions, she explains that online AI is needed.

      An administrator can set a provider, model, and key in Flower settings. The key is encrypted on the server. External model replies require a server and valid configuration; the static preview uses the local FAQ.

    2. Newv2026.11
      November 15, 2026

      Edit your files and review the result

      PDF, image, audio, video, document, presentation, and spreadsheet editors are now available.

      Read update

      The PDF editor adds and positions text, images, lines, and rectangles. The image editor now offers text, shape, watermark, and image layers, with undo and redo.

      Audio and video editors support trimming, speed, volume, other adjustments, export, and playback of the edited result. The media engine loads on first export; larger files may need more time or memory.

      The Office editors open DOCX/TXT, PPTX, and XLSX/CSV. Change text, apply basic formatting, organize slides, and edit cells or formulas. Office previews are simplified; formulas recalculate in compatible applications. Legacy formats and macros are not supported.

      These editors process files in your browser. Search “editor” on the Tools page to find them.

    3. Newv2026.11
      November 14, 2026

      A place to follow what changed

      The portal now has an update history and a notification center with more details.

      Read update

      Follow new features, improvements, and fixes here. Use the category filters and open an entry for the details.

      A notification opens its details before taking you to the page. You can mark everything as read, clear current notifications, or turn notifications off. These preferences are saved on this device.

    4. Improvementv2026.11
      November 14, 2026

      Excel spreadsheets that are easier to review

      Readable headers, alternating rows, and adjusted columns help you review exported data.

      Read update

      Spreadsheets now use clear header contrast, alternating rows, light borders, and wrapping for longer text.

      Professional reports offer filters on the Data sheet and frozen headers. Numeric values, formulas, and cell positions are preserved. Very small values may use scientific notation to stay visible.

    5. Improvementv2026.11
      November 14, 2026

      Generation animations lasting 10 to 20 seconds

      The presentation lasts 10, 15, or 20 seconds depending on the tool, respects reduced motion, and makes results available as soon as generation finishes.

      Read update

      The generation presentation has a minimum duration of 10, 15, or 20 seconds depending on the tool, and respects the device's reduced motion preference.

      The result and download are available as soon as processing finishes. The completion scene may continue until its planned duration ends, without delaying access to the file.

    6. Improvementv2026.11
      November 14, 2026

      Receipts and proposals with your visual identity

      Add your business identity to documents and review them before downloading.

      Read update

      You can now add your business visual identity to PDF receipts and proposals.

      The preview shows the document before download so you can review its details and presentation.

    7. Newv2026.11
      November 14, 2026

      Financial analysis with NPV, IRR, and sensitivity

      Review metrics calculated from your cash flows and compare sensitivity scenarios.

      Read update

      The financial analysis calculates NPV and IRR from the cash flows you provide and presents sensitivity scenarios.

      Results reflect your inputs and assumptions so you can compare alternatives with the calculations in view.
